Problem

Why it matters
right now

Microplastics are already found in human blood, breast milk and in the air. Yet systematic groundwater monitoring is almost non-existent.

🔬
Expensive analysis methods
Existing technologies (FTIR, Raman spectroscopy) require expensive lab equipment and specialists. One sample can take weeks.
📍
No field tools
Volunteers and ecologists lack affordable portable devices for on-site water analysis. Everything is sent to a lab.
🗺️
Source tracing is hard
Even when microplastic is detected, it is hard to know the source: landfill, factory, or road? Without data, there is no response.
📊
No open data
The public does not know the real condition of local water. The issue stays “invisible” until an ecological disaster.
How it works

Automatic cycle
every 2 weeks

The system works without human involvement. Data accumulates and is sent to the platform for trend analysis.

01
🚿
Water intake
A peristaltic pump draws exactly 1 liter of water from the source. Fixed volume is critical for comparable measurements (C = N/V).
Intake System
02
🔩
Filtration
Water passes through a 5–20 µm fiberglass microfilter. A coarse 300–500 µm pre-filter removes leaves and sand.
Filtration Module
03
📷
Macro imaging
A USB microscope photographs the filter under ring LED illumination. Controlled lighting is key to AI analysis quality.
Imaging Module
04
🤖
AI analysis
A Raspberry Pi algorithm counts particles, classifies them by shape (fibers, fragments), and sends the data to the cloud.
Computer Vision
Architecture

Full cycle
in 10 minutes

The system sleeps most of the time. Every 14 days it activates, takes a measurement, and returns to sleep mode to save energy.

Contamination Control

Inside — metal, glass, and PTFE tubing. The system self-tests with ultra-pure water (blank tests) to account for background contamination.

Clear limitations

The system does not replace FTIR spectroscopy. The result is “probable microplastic” for long-term trend tracking, not chemical analysis.

Energy autonomy

Solar panel + battery. 10 minutes of operation every 2 weeks equals months of autonomous operation without maintenance.

🔬
Technologies

Solution stack

Components are selected with a balance between accuracy, cost and system autonomy.

🔬
Imaging & Optics
USB microscope or Raspberry Pi Camera module with macro lens. Ring LED illumination for even lighting. Optional UV + Nile Red for fluorescence detection.
USB Microscope Ring LED UV Fluorescence
🧠
Computer Vision
Pipeline: grayscale → contrast enhancement → thresholding → feature extraction (shape, size, texture) → ML classification. Models: Random Forest for MVP, CNN (U-Net) for advanced version.
OpenCV Random Forest U-Net CNN
⚙️
Hardware & Control
A Raspberry Pi controls the whole system: pump, lighting, camera and AI. Data is sent via LTE/LoRa or stored on an SD card. Power is solar panel + LiPo battery.
